Scope, evidence & provenance

A research map, not a verdict

What belongs here?

Experimental screening, sequence mining, directed evolution, protein language models, active learning, structure-based design and molecular simulation. AI is a method dimension—not an inclusion requirement. Discovery can concern natural or generated sequences. Objectives are non-exclusive; pathway engineering alone is not automatically enzyme engineering.

What do the labels mean?

Source-checked seed means an AI-assisted selection linked to primary publication records. Human-reviewed requires a named reviewer, decision reason and review basis. Candidate means automatically retrieved and not yet accepted. “Experimental assays reported” describes the study, not every sequence or prediction within it.

How are updates handled?

Europe PMC retrieval works without a paid model. OpenAlex and model-assisted tagging are optional. Date-window searches, stable identifiers, persistent decisions and per-query retrieval logs support incremental updates and historical backfills. Truncation and failed searches are never silently reported as complete.

Live retrieval has not been run for this release. The starting papers were source-checked individually; no scheduled service is claimed to be active.

What is not claimed?

This starting collection is not exhaustive, a completed systematic review or a calibrated ranking of research quality. Source abstracts and metadata have been checked, but there has been no independent full-text review of every paper. Kinetic results across different assays are not combined into a league table.

Review without hidden cloud writes

Shortlists and draft review decisions stay in your browser. Open a paper to record a reviewer, reason and decision. Export the decisions and import them into the versioned dataset. Draft decisions do not alter the published collection until that import is committed and rebuilt.
